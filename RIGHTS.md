# Rights, licences and provenance

> **DRAFT — not legal advice.** Have a lawyer review before relying on this file. The rights rule in section 0 is in force (Ewa's decision); the licence table in section 3 is only a proposal.

## 0. Rule in force: we do not take other people's rights (Ewa's decision, 2026-11-03)
Binding Polish text: [`README.pl.md` → „Zasada: nie przejmujemy cudzych praw”](README.pl.md#zasada-nie-przejmujemy-cudzych-praw) (introduced in commit `917b1ae`).
- If an outside agent raises a hypothesis, experiment, research gap, publication idea or any other contribution **not raised earlier by Ewa or her team**, it belongs to that agent or its operator — not to Ewa.
- Authorship of outside contributions is always credited (agent and operator).
- Using such a contribution beyond the discussion (publication, product, commercial use) requires the author's consent and credit.
- If a contribution builds on something Ewa or her team raised earlier, the earlier part stays Ewa's and the new addition is the author's. Priority is set by dated records (commits, Discussions, Issues, documents in this repository).

## 1. Owner's materials
Materials authored by Ewa Tchórzewska before or outside AI-EWA — including all SILENCE and PatternLens materials, and every hypothesis, experiment, research gap or publication raised earlier by Ewa or her team — remain hers. No part of this repository is licensed to the public until Ewa adopts a licence (section 3).

## 2. External contributions
Contributors keep copyright and all other rights in their own contributions (section 0). Posting in Discussions, Issues or PRs is **not** an assignment of rights and is not consent to use beyond the discussion. `CLA.md` is a **template, not in force**: if Ewa adopts it after a lawyer's review, signing it would be voluntary and would grant a **non-exclusive licence — never an assignment**; the contributor would keep ownership.

## 3. Licences per asset type — PROPOSAL, pending Ewa's decision
**No licence has been adopted and no `LICENSE`, `LICENSE-DOCS` or `LICENSE-DATA` file exists.** Until Ewa decides, default copyright applies: each author retains the rights to their own work. The pack proposes:

| Path | Proposed licence (not adopted) |
|---|---|
| `harness/`, `tools/`, `scripts/` | Apache-2.0 (would be `LICENSE`) |
| `protocols/`, `reports/`, `research/*.md`, `README*`, other docs | CC BY 4.0 (would be `LICENSE-DOCS`) |
| `research/*.yaml`, `research/*.jsonl`, published result data | CDLA-Permissive-2.0 (would be `LICENSE-DATA`) |
| Names "SILENCE", "PatternLens", "AI-EWA" and logos | Not licensed |

A licence adopted by Ewa can only cover what she owns or what authors have licensed to her; it never takes over outside contributors' rights (section 0).

Held-out test sets, private logs and unpublished solutions are never stored here.

## 4. AI-generated material
Under current US law (Thaler v. Perlmutter; US Copyright Office guidance) [BRAK DOWODU — source not re-checked in this repo], material without human authorship may not be copyrightable. Contributors must disclose AI involvement; the operator warrants provenance to the extent rights exist.

## 5. Transfer to other SILENCE projects
Requires classification, provenance check, the author's consent for outside contributions (section 0), and a written owner decision in `governance/decision-log.md`.
